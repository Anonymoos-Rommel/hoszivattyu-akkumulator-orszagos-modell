# B02-P114 — EMPIRICAL OFP APPLICATION ACTION-MIX ANCHOR

## Goal

P113 left the completed KEHOP 4.1.7/4.1.8 1103 readback unresolved.

The next slice was explicitly constrained not to add another semantic FAIR
boundary. P114 therefore admits only a real programme observation.

## 1. Actual programme snapshot

Portfolio asked Kasziba Levente, Director of the MFB EU Business Directorate,
for the current position of the MFB-coordinated energy-efficiency
Otthonfelújítási Program.

For the **2025-10-17** snapshot he reported:

- **HUF 50.4bn** applications;
- **HUF 40.3bn** approvals;
- **HUF 18.8bn** disbursements;
- disbursement affecting **4,457** applications;
- **HUF 5.5m** average application;
- **90%** of applications included insulation;
- in **approximately two thirds** of applications insulation and window
  replacement appeared together.

This is actual observed programme behaviour, not a planned measure menu or a
data-system capability statement.

## 2. Independent secondary confirmation

The November 2025 MEHI newsletter repeats the same programme action-mix data,
citing the MFB/Portfolio update:

- insulation in 90% of applications;
- insulation + window replacement together in roughly two thirds.

This is a useful independent publication crosscheck, but the MFB director's
reported figures remain the primary provenance used by P114.

## 3. Exact scope boundary

The Portfolio source explains that the energy-efficiency OFP began in July 2024
with RRF resources and that in January 2025 uncommitted resources were moved
into the KEHOP Plusz programme.

The reported October-2025 programme statistics are presented as the cumulative
programme series.

P114 therefore binds the observation to:

RRF_PLUS_KEHOP_CUMULATIVE_OFP

and does **not** claim that the action mix is a clean
KEHOP Plusz-4.1.7-24 / 4.1.8-24-only extract.

## 4. Stage boundary

The action mix is explicitly reported for **applications**.

Therefore:

APPLICATION ACTION MIX != COMPLETED ACTION COHORT

The 4,457 applications with disbursement are financial-stage observations.
They are not silently relabelled physically completed projects.

## 5. Window label versus administrative 1103

The reported source uses the human-readable action
nyílászárócsere.

P109 independently establishes that MEKH category 1103 is the administrative
window-replacement category used by the KEHOP completion schema.

P114 does not collapse those two evidence grains:

WINDOW REPLACEMENT LABEL != VERIFIED FAIR/MEKH 1103 READBACK

The source does not expose a project-level FAIR category field or an exact
1103 selection.

## 6. What the observation does establish

The programme data are strong evidence that window replacement was not a
marginal or hypothetical action among actual applicants.

At least the reported combined class — approximately two thirds of applications
— contained both insulation and window replacement.

Because the source says "approximately two thirds", P114 does not convert this
into an exact hard 66.6667% statistical bound.

The observation is admitted for:

- programme-behaviour calibration;
- action-mix plausibility checking;
- validation of future exact 1103 cohort outputs.

It is prohibited for:

- national housing-stock prevalence;
- completed-project 1103 share;
- window-U compliance;
- technical post-state distribution;
- KEHOP-only action share without a source-native phase split.

## 7. Completion blocker remains

P114 is genuine empirical progress, but the exact P113 blocker remains:

KEHOP_417_418_PROGRAMMONITORING_1103_INCLUSION_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_EXPORT_READBACK_REQUIRED

The preferred record route also remains:

FAIR_IH_KEHOP_1103_COMPLETION_RECORD_EXTRACT_REQUIRED

The next useful evidence must isolate the KEHOP 4.1.7/4.1.8 phase and/or expose
the physically completed administrative 1103 field.
