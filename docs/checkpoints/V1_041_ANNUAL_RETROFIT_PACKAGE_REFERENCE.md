# V1-041: a connected annual retrofit-package reference

The same historical 56 m² SFH01 building now has an annual original, standard
and ambitious package comparison using the existing WM50/W55 method and exact
UTC 2025 weather. The source packages retain common nominal service inputs and
geometry. They change envelope, infiltration and distribution/system details;
this is neither insulation-only savings nor an identical measured indoor
temperature trajectory.

## Common source convention and qualified transfers

The original workbook building rows 1239–1241 use the same 56 m² area, nominal
20°C input, user ventilation, source climate and annual method. The source
nonuniform-heating function responds to transmission loss: its factors are
0.8, 0.8605286120 and 0.8856106006. Infiltration changes from 0.4 to 0.1 h⁻¹ in
both renovated packages. These source responses are preserved, rather than
relabelled as new national comfort choices or measured room-temperature paths.

The native effective distribution loss is 12.5 kWh/m² for the original package
and 5.4 kWh/m² for both renovated packages; storage is zero in these source
references. Their transfer to fixed-W55 operation remains explicit E2 debt.
Each historical annual useful/distribution total is preserved when allocated
onto the same observed weather. These totals are not observed or
weather-corrected 2025 building consumption.

The existing paired-power, equivalent MIN-stage default and 15 W nonactive
method are unchanged. All source annotations survive: mixed grey/unmarked
defrost-support hours are 2,825, 1,638 and 58, respectively. There is no new
universal defrost multiplier or assertion that unmarked supports are
defrost-free. Greater exposure to those supports remains visible in the
provisional reference and its validation debt.

## Original gas to a complete renovated HP package

Original system row 3390 supplies 21,085.568 kWh_GCV/year of source-allocated space
heating gas and 168.56 kWh/year of heating auxiliary electricity. The standard
and ambitious source gas accounts are 8,891.582 and 6,353.082 kWh_GCV/year, with
156.8 kWh/year auxiliaries each. Their different gas factors and distribution
losses mean these gas-package differences are not pure envelope effects.

The public path requires an explicit source-package/full-space-heating
replacement SCN condition and a standard or ambitious after-case. It uses
the original gas baseline, not the renovated package's alternative gas system.
For signed installed-minus-rating reconciliations A_standard or A_ambitious,
its annual reference electricity changes are:

- Original gas → standard HP: `3,177.176 + A_standard` kWh
- Original gas → ambitious HP: `2,304.074 + A_ambitious` kWh

Each reference condition displaces the original source allocation of
21,085.568 kWh_GCV. It is not measured whole-boiler savings or evidence of actual
equipment retirement. The source heating auxiliary allocation is not a
measurement of removable hardware consumption. Residuals include the electricity
consequences of matching installed heat delivery/duty, replacement of embedded
pump conventions and genuinely additional/excluded or retained/reallocated
combi controls. They can have either sign and are not bounded or silently
cancelled. The displayed intercepts are not lower bounds on complete changes.

The exact EN14511-3:2013 pump convention affects effective electrical input
and rated heating capacity. The manufacturer explicitly includes a nominal
W35 pump allowance of 10 W; that is not a fixed allowance established for every
W55 operating point. EN14511-1:2013 includes all control/safety input belonging
to the rated unit. Those devices need neither a guessed extra term nor another
submeter. Equipment genuinely excluded from the tested boundary and retained
boiler demand remain relevant to the signed reconciliation.

This clarification changes no source-rating calculation, profile or numerical
default. Source-reference service completion remains separate from actual
installed heat delivery/duty, which is still unestablished. The two exact
standard previews are recorded as
`SRC-B05-EN14511-3-2013-SIST-PREVIEW` and
`SRC-B05-EN14511-1-2013-SIST-PREVIEW`; originals remain external. The initial
reviewed/tested candidate is preserved as superseded evidence; final review
and validation bind the corrected semantic metadata.

All DHW effects are outside this comparison, including the source package's
solar or DHW-appliance changes. The consumer uses CI space-heating gas and BW
heating auxiliaries, preserving the previous correction for DHW-inclusive
DQ/DU labels. Seasonal gas and auxiliary scalars do not supply hourly boiler
or auxiliary profiles. Complete net electricity, gas volume, import value,
household savings, physical SPF and national results remain unavailable.
