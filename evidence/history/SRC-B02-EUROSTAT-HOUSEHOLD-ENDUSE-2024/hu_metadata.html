15.1. Comparability - geographical

Data are compiled according to the rules set out in Regulation (EC) 1099/2008 on energy statistics, which ensured the comparability within the EU.

15.2. Comparability - over time

The data are generally comparable over time. There are some breaks in time series due to methodological changes:

  • Residential solid biomass consumption data were revised in 2015 and time-series were back-casted to 2005.
  • There is a break in the time series of final energy consumption of Industry and Commercial and Public Services in 2013 due to the change of the data collection and estimation methodology.

Energy statistics data can be compared to others statistical domains, e.g. Foreign Trade Statistics or Physical Energy Flow Accounts Statistics by taking into account the different methodologies.
15.3.1. Coherence - sub annual and annual statistics

Monthly data are comparable to Annual data taking into account the different methodologies (i.e. the treatment of transit, etc.). Monthly data are in general not revised after the release of Annual data.

Energy statistics is using the General Business Register maintained by Hungarian Central Statistical Office as a sampling frame and also for determining the NACE activity of the enterprises, which ensures good consistency with National Accounts.

Statistical outputs are internally consistent.
